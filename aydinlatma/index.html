Ball Merge Classic: Privacy Notice (KVKK)

Version 1 · Last updated: 28 October 2026

1. Data controller

Alperen Somuncuoğlu (Alplay). Contact: alpisomun61@gmail.com. A postal address is provided on request by e-mail.

2. Data, purposes and legal bases

  • Ads (only if you consent): advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, ad interactions. Purpose: showing ads, measurement, fraud prevention. Legal basis: explicit consent. Recipient: Google LLC (USA).
  • Online leaderboard (if you open it): anonymous player ID, session information, IP address, your best score and an automatically generated player name. Your score and name are visible to other players. Purpose: operating the leaderboard. Legal basis: performance of a contract (KVKK Art. 5/2-c). Recipient: Unity Technologies (USA).
  • Data that stays on your device: language, settings, records, achievements. This is not sent to us.
  • The in-app rating prompt is handled by Google Play.

3. Transfers abroad

The data above is transferred to Google and Unity servers in the USA. For ad data, the transfer is based on your explicit consent. Leaderboard data is transferred as part of performing the service.

4. Retention and deletion

Your consent record is kept only on your device. Ad data is retained under Google’s own policies. The in-app “Delete my online data” option deletes your anonymous online account and the session information on your device. However, your score and automatic player name may remain visible on the leaderboard. You can ask for them to be deleted too by writing to alpisomun61@gmail.com; the entry is removed manually.

5. Your rights (KVKK Art. 11)

You may learn whether your data is processed, request information, correction, deletion, and object. Submit requests in writing, by registered e-mail (KEP), with a secure electronic signature, or from your e-mail address registered in our system, in line with the applicable Communiqué. As an extra convenience you may also write to alpisomun61@gmail.com. Requests are answered free of charge within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Personal Data Protection Board.

At any time from Settings > Ads.
